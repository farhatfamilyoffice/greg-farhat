About Sheri

Three decades of Greater Lansing, in her own words.

"Full-time agent, licensed in 1991 with Century 21. Joined RE/MAX in 2011. I attend regional and national seminars and conferences every year — to keep getting better at serving my clients."

Behind that quiet line: 676 closings across thirty-six years, 72 of them in the last twelve months alone. Clients range from first-time buyers writing their first offer to families selling a generations-old farm — and they consistently come back, then send their kids.

Sheri is recognized as a 2026 Top Agent in Lansing, East Lansing, and Okemos, holds Hall of Fame status from her two decades at Century 21, and carries nearly every meaningful designation in residential real estate.
