Dad's Sloppy Joes

Ground turkey or beef with chicken gumbo soup, mustard & ketchup — served on Kaiser rolls

Section
Meats
Cook Time
~30 min
Serves
6–8

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey or beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 can chicken gumbo soup
  • 1 T mustard
  • 2 T ketchup
  • Tabasco or Red Hot to taste
  • Worcestershire sauce to taste
  • Kaiser rolls, for serving

Directions

  1. Brown ground meat with chopped onion in a skillet. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add chicken gumbo soup, mustard, and ketchup. Stir to combine.
  3. Add Tabasco or Red Hot and Worcestershire sauce to taste.
  4. Simmer on medium-low heat for about 20 minutes until thickened.
  5. Serve on Kaiser rolls.
Dad's Note: Chicken gumbo soup is the secret ingredient — it gives the sauce a depth and texture you can't get anywhere else.
