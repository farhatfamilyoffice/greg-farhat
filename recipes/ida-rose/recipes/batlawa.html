Batlawa

Virginia Farhat's Lebanese baklava — phyllo, walnuts & orange blossom syrup

Oven
300°F
Bake
60–90 min
Servings
10+
Category
Lebanese · Dessert

Ingredients

Batlawa

  • 3 lbs butter (rendered — see tip)
  • 2–3 lbs walnuts, ground (not too fine)
  • 1½–2 cups granulated sugar (use 2 cups if using 3 lbs nuts)
  • 2 packages fresh phyllo dough, at room temperature
  • 1 capful Mazaher (orange blossom water)

Syrup

  • 4 cups granulated sugar
  • 1½–1¾ cups water
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1–3 tbsp Mazaher (orange blossom water), to taste
Rendering butter: Melt butter slowly over low heat. The white foam (whey) will rise to the top — skim it off. Use only the clear golden liquid below.
Nut ratio: 2 lbs nuts → 1¼ cups sugar. 3 lbs nuts → 2 cups sugar.
Critical rule: The syrup must be completely cold before pouring over the hot batlawa. Make the syrup first so it has time to cool.

Directions

Make the syrup first — it must cool completely

  1. Combine 4 cups sugar and 1½–1¾ cups water in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Stir until the sugar dissolves and the mixture comes to a clear boil.
  2. Add 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice. Stir and simmer on low heat for 10–15 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside to cool completely. It will thicken as it cools. Once off the stove, stir in the Mazaher (orange blossom water). Do not refrigerate.

Assemble the batlawa

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
  2. Pour ½ cup of rendered butter into the bottom of a large baking pan. Tilt to coat the bottom evenly.
  3. Open the first package of phyllo dough. Layer each sheet one at a time into the pan, brushing each sheet generously with rendered butter before adding the next. Work quickly — phyllo dries out fast. Keep the unused sheets covered with a damp towel.
  4. Continue until the entire first package of phyllo is layered in the pan.
  5. Mix the ground walnuts with the sugar and 1 capful of Mazaher. Spread this nut mixture evenly over the phyllo base. Drizzle a little extra rendered butter over the nut layer.
  6. Open the second package of phyllo. Layer each sheet over the nut filling, again brushing each layer with butter, until the second package is fully used.
  7. Fold any overhanging edges of phyllo up and into the sides of the pan, pressing gently to seal.
  8. Pour the remaining rendered butter evenly over the top of the final phyllo layer. Make sure the entire surface is covered.
  9. Using a sharp knife, score the batlawa into diamond shapes all the way through — cut diagonally in two directions across the pan.
  10. Bake at 300°F on a lower oven rack for 60–90 minutes, until the top is light golden brown. Check after 60 minutes.
  11. Remove from oven and set the pan on a rack. Let it rest for 3 minutes.
  12. Pour the cold syrup evenly over the hot batlawa. It will sizzle. Let it soak in fully before serving.
Storing: Batlawa keeps at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, covered loosely. Do not refrigerate — it will lose its crispness.
