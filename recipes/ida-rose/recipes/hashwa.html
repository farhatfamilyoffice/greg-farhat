Hashwa

Spiced ground beef & rice — the heart of Lebanese stuffing

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Servings
4–6
Category
Lebanese · Syrian

Ingredients

Hashwa

  • 1 lb ground beef (round or sirloin)
  • 2 cups Uncle Ben's original converted rice (or other long-grain rice)
  • 1 stick butter
  • 1 tsp salt (level)
  • 1 tsp Syrian spice (level) — see below
  • ¼ cup pine nuts, toasted in a dry skillet
  • 4 cups chicken broth or 4 cups water + 4 chicken bouillon cubes (Wyler's)

Syrian Spice Blend

  • 2 oz Mezji (or black pepper)
  • 2 × 4 oz containers allspice
  • ⅛ tsp cinnamon
Syrian Spice: Mix all spices together, store in a jar in the fridge. Use 1 level teaspoon per recipe. This blend is the backbone of Farhat family cooking.

Directions

  1. In a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  2. Add the butter, salt, and Syrian spice. Stir to combine.
  3. Add the chicken broth (or water with bouillon cubes). Stir and bring the mixture to a full boil.
  4. Add the rice. Stir to blend everything together. Cover the pot.
  5. Reduce heat to low. Simmer for 20 minutes — do not lift the lid.
  6. Turn off the heat. Remove the cover and stir the rice mixture to fluff. Replace the cover and leave on the warm stove for an additional 5–10 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork. Fold in the toasted pine nuts and serve.
How to use Hashwa: Serve as a side dish on its own, or use as a stuffing inside cabbage rolls, grape leaves, or zucchini. It's the base stuffing for most Lebanese stuffed vegetable dishes.
Toasting pine nuts: Add pine nuts to a dry (no oil) skillet over medium heat. Stir constantly for 2–3 minutes until golden. Watch carefully — they burn fast.
