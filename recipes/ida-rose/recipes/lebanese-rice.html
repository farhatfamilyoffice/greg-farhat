Lebanese Rice

Buttery rice toasted with golden orzo — simple, perfect, essential

Prep
15 min
Cook
20–30 min
Servings
4–6
Category
Lebanese

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup orzo or vermicelli, broken into ¼-inch pieces
  • 4 cups water
Note: Orzo or vermicelli can be found at Middle Eastern import stores. Either works — vermicelli gives a slightly finer texture.
Serve with: This rice is the classic pairing for Yahnee (Lebanese stewed lamb) and pairs equally well alongside Cabbage Rolls or Hashwa.

Directions

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water, swirling with your hand, until the water runs completely clear. Drain well.
  2. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the orzo or broken vermicelli pieces.
  3. Stir constantly until the pasta turns a deep golden brown — about 3–4 minutes. Watch carefully; it goes from golden to burnt quickly.
  4. Add the drained rice to the pan and stir gently to coat it in the butter and mix with the toasted pasta.
  5. Add the water and salt. Stir once, then bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  6. Once boiling, reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover tightly and cook until all the water is absorbed — about 20 to 30 minutes. Do not lift the lid during cooking.
  7. Remove from heat. Let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and serve.
Getting it right: The key is toasting the pasta until it's a rich amber color before adding the rice. That browning is what gives Lebanese rice its distinctive nutty flavor.
