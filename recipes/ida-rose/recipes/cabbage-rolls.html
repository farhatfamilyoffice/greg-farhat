Cabbage Rolls

Ida Farhat's signature — lamb and rice rolled in tender cabbage leaves

Prep
1 hour
Cook
1 hour
Servings
4–6
Yield
~40 rolls
Category
Lebanese

Ingredients

Stuffing

  • 1 lb lamb or beef, coarsely ground
  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 stick margarine (or butter)
  • 1 tbsp Syrian spice (or allspice)
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup water (to loosen mixture)

Cabbage & Pot

  • 1 head of cabbage
  • Lamb bones or country-style pork ribs (optional, for pot)
  • Juice of 2 large lemons
  • Dried mint leaves, crushed
  • 2 cloves garlic, halved (or garlic powder)
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 2 cups water (plus liquid from boiled cabbage)
Ida's Note: Use lamb or pork (country-style ribs) stock first, then add the liquid from the boiled cabbage and cover to the top of the cabbage rolls.
Yield Guide: 1 head of cabbage ≈ 40 rolls. Half a head ≈ 40 rolls when leaves are medium-sized.

Directions

  1. Combine all stuffing ingredients — ground meat, rice, margarine, salt, pepper, and Syrian spice. Add approximately ½ cup of water and mix well until the mixture is loose and wet. Add more water if needed. Set aside.
  2. Carve out the core from the bottom of the cabbage head. Place whole cabbage in a large pot of boiling water. Boil for a few minutes until outer leaves begin to soften. As each leaf softens, use a fork to loosen and remove it. Stack leaves on a plate to cool.
  3. Cut away the thick center stem from each leaf, slicing down the middle so you have two flat pieces per leaf.
  4. Place about 1 tablespoon of stuffing across the bottom of each leaf piece. Roll into a firm cigar shape, tucking in the sides as you go.
  5. Line the bottom of a heavy pot with lamb bones, pork ribs, or reserved cabbage stems. Arrange rolls in tight rows across the pot, alternating direction in each layer. Sprinkle each layer lightly with salt, pepper, and crushed dried mint.
  6. Tuck garlic clove halves (or sprinkle garlic powder) between the rolls. Place a heavy plate on top of the rolls to keep them from opening during cooking.
  7. Add 2 cups of water (or stock) — enough to come to the top of the rolls. Bring to a rolling boil over high heat, then lower to medium-low. Cover and cook 45 minutes.
  8. Add the juice of two large lemons. Bring back to a boil, then reduce heat and cook an additional 15 minutes.
  9. Serve warm with pita bread.
Serving Suggestion: Cabbage rolls are even better the next day as the flavors deepen. Reheat gently with a splash of water or broth.
