White Wine & Peach Sangria

From Bon Appétit January 1996, with Ida's handwritten additions

Yield
6 cups
Chill
2 hours
Section
Beverages

Ingredients

Base

  • 1 bottle (750ml) dry white wine
  • ¾ cup peach brandy or peach schnapps
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ½ (16 oz) package frozen unsweetened sliced peaches
  • ¾ cup seedless green grapes, halved
  • ¾ cup seedless red grapes, halved

Ida's Additions

  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • ¾ liter chilled Seagram's ginger ale

Directions

  1. Stir white wine, peach brandy (or schnapps), and sugar in a large pitcher until sugar dissolves.
  2. Add frozen peaches, green grapes, red grapes, orange slices, and lemon slices.
  3. Refrigerate until well chilled, at least 2 hours.
  4. Just before serving, stir in chilled ginger ale.
  5. Serve over ice.
Ida's note: The orange and lemon slices and ginger ale are her additions — they lighten and brighten the sangria beautifully.
