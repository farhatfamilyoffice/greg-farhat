Ravioli Soup

Ida's quick weeknight soup — ravioli in a rich tomato-arrabbiata broth

Category
Soups
Serves
3 generous servings

Ida Farhat's recipe — a family favorite

Ingredients

  • 12 oz pkg frozen cheese and spinach ravioli (Buitoni 4-cheese also works)
  • 15 oz can chicken broth (Swanson's Natural Goodness)
  • 20 oz jar Rao's Arrabbiata pasta sauce
  • 15 oz can Hunt's Tomato Sauce
  • 5 TBSP Parmesan Reggiano or Pecorino Romano (or half and half)
  • 5 TBSP fresh ricotta

Directions

  1. Combine chicken broth, Rao's Arrabbiata sauce, and Hunt's Tomato Sauce in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat until heated through.
  2. Meanwhile, cook ravioli separately in boiling water according to package directions.
  3. Add drained ravioli to the heated sauce. If too thick, loosen with a little pasta water.
  4. Stir in cheeses once sauce is hot enough to melt them. Mix until melted.
  5. Serve immediately.
