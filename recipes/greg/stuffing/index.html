Stuffing

Sausage and mushroom stuffing — a Thanksgiving staple

Category
Sides
Serves
10–12

Ingredients

  • 10 slices white bread (or small bag bread crumbs)
  • ½ stick butter
  • 1 cup celery, chopped
  • 2 cups onions, diced
  • 2 cups mushrooms, chopped large
  • 1 lb Jimmy Dean regular sausage
  • 3/8 TBSP salt
  • 3/8 TBSP pepper
  • ¾ TBSP sage
  • 2 eggs
  • 2¼ cups milk

Directions

  1. Toast and cube bread (or use pre-made bread crumbs).
  2. Cook sausage until browned; drain.
  3. Sauté celery first, then add onions; cook 10–15 minutes with ½ stick butter. Add mushrooms; cook 5 more minutes.
  4. Combine bread/crumbs, sausage, sautéed vegetables, salt, pepper, sage, eggs, and milk; mix well.
  5. Butter a baking dish (bottom and sides). Add stuffing mixture. Cover with wax paper sprayed with butter or PAM.
  6. Bake at 375° for 1.5 hours. Remove wax paper and cook an additional 10–15 minutes until top is golden.
