Pulled Pork

Slow-cooker pork shoulder with Coke and BBQ sauce

Category
Mains
Serves
8–10

Ingredients

  • 1 whole pork shoulder roast
  • 12 oz Coca-Cola
  • 1 TBSP onion powder
  • 1 TBSP garlic powder
  • 1 TBSP black pepper
  • 1 TBSP kosher salt
  • 1 bottle BBQ sauce

Directions

  1. Remove any thick slabs of fat from the outside of the roast.
  2. Whisk spices together and rub over the entire surface of the roast.
  3. Place roast in slow cooker. Pour Coke and half the BBQ sauce over the top.
  4. Cover and cook on high for 5 hours (or low for 10 hours) until meat falls apart.
  5. Remove roast; discard bones. Shred meat with two forks.
  6. Return shredded meat to slow cooker. Add half the reserved cooking juices and all remaining BBQ sauce; mix well.
  7. Set on low, cover, and cook 30 more minutes before serving.
