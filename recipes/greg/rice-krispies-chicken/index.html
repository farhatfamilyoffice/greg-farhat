Rice Krispies Chicken

Crispy baked chicken coated in seasoned crushed Rice Krispies

Category
Mains
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 3–4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 4 cups Rice Krispies cereal
  • 1 stick butter
  • ½ TBSP salt (consider reducing)
  • ½ TBSP pepper
  • ½ tsp granulated garlic powder
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°.
  2. Melt butter in a bowl.
  3. Spray a 13×9 Pyrex baking dish with cooking spray (or line with parchment paper).
  4. Place Rice Krispies in a zip-lock bag and crush with a rolling pin. Add salt, pepper, and garlic powder; shake to combine.
  5. Dip each chicken breast in melted butter, then roll in crushed cereal to coat well.
  6. Place in baking dish. Sprinkle remaining cereal and spices over the top; drizzle remaining butter over chicken.
  7. Sprinkle paprika lightly over the top.
  8. Bake uncovered 50–60 minutes until Rice Krispies are golden brown. Note: internal temp reaches 165° before the crust turns golden — go by color.
