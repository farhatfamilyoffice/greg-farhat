Cheeseball

Three-cheese party classic with a kick

Category
Appetizers
Serves
A crowd

Ingredients

  • 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese
  • 2 (4 oz) Kraft Old English Cheddar Cheese
  • 2 (6 oz) packages bleu cheese
  • 3 drops Tabasco
  • ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
  • ¼ cup red wine
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Sliced Spanish olives (for topping)
  • Triscuits and Wheat Thins (for serving)

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients except olives together until well combined.
  2. Form into a ball.
  3. Top with sliced Spanish olives.
  4. Serve with Triscuits and Wheat Thins.
