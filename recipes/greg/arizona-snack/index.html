Arizona Snack

Hot sausage and Velveeta on cocktail bread — an easy crowd-pleaser

Category
Appetizers
Serves
A crowd

Ingredients

  • 1 lb hot sausage
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb Velveeta cheese
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1½ TBSP oregano
  • 1 package Pepperidge Farm cocktail bread

Directions

  1. Brown sausage and ground beef together; drain fat.
  2. Add Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, and oregano; mix well.
  3. Cut Velveeta into cubes and fold in over medium heat until melted and combined.
  4. Spread mixture onto cocktail bread slices and serve.
