Pot Roast

Chuck roast braised with whole onions, potatoes, and carrots

Category
Mains

Ingredients

  • Chuck roast
  • Black pepper
  • Granulated garlic
  • Lawry's seasoned salt
  • Water
  • 2–3 whole white or yellow onions (peeled, bottom left intact)
  • Potatoes
  • Carrots

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°.
  2. Season roast generously with pepper, granulated garlic, and Lawry's.
  3. Brown roast on both sides in a Dutch oven or roasting pot.
  4. Reduce oven to 350°. Add water to the height of the roast. Cover and roast for 2 hours.
  5. Add whole peeled onions (keep bottom intact); cook 45 more minutes.
  6. Add potatoes and carrots; cook a final 45 minutes.
