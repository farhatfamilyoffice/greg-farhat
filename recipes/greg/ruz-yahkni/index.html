Ruz Yahkni — Lebanese Rice

Orzo-toasted Lebanese rice cooked in chicken broth

Category
Lebanese & Syrian

Ingredients

  • Chicken stock
  • Butter
  • Orzo pasta
  • Long-grain white rice, washed
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°.
  2. Bring chicken stock to a boil in a separate pot.
  3. In an oven-safe pan, melt butter over medium heat. Stir in orzo and cook until golden brown. Remove pan from heat.
  4. Pour a ladle of hot broth into the pan with the orzo.
  5. Meanwhile, wash rice a couple of times; add salt after washing.
  6. Combine rice, salted orzo, and enough stock to cover. Cover tightly with foil.
  7. Bake at 350° for 70 minutes.
