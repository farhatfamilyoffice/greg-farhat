French Dip Roll-Ups

Caramelized onion, roast beef, and provolone in flaky crescent dough — with au jus

Category
Appetizers
Serves
6

Ingredients

For the Roll-Ups

  • 1 tube crescent roll dough
  • 3 tbsp mustard
  • 1 lb deli roast beef
  • 12 slices provolone cheese
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp flaky sea salt
  • 1 tbsp parsley, finely chopped

For the Au Jus

  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1½ cups beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ¼ tsp thyme leaves

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°. Grease an 8"×8" baking pan with cooking spray.
  2. On a lightly floured surface, unroll dough, separate into rectangles, and pinch perforations to seal. Spread mustard over rectangles; set aside.
  3. Caramelize onions: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, melt butter and stir in onions and thyme sprigs. Cook, stirring occasionally, until onions begin to soften. Season with salt and pepper; reduce to medium and continue cooking until soft and caramelized.
  4. Make roll-ups: Layer roast beef and cheese on each rectangle, then top with caramelized onions. Roll up each rectangle starting from one short side; pinch edges to seal. Brush with melted butter; sprinkle with garlic powder, sea salt, and parsley. Cut each roll into 5–6 slices. Place cut side up in baking pan.
  5. Bake until dough is slightly golden and cheese is melty, 15–18 minutes.
  6. Make au jus: Melt butter in a medium skillet over medium heat. Add garlic; cook until fragrant, about 5 minutes. Add beef stock, Worcestershire, and thyme; season with salt and pepper. Simmer until reduced, about 10 minutes.
  7. Garnish with parsley and serve with au jus for dipping.
