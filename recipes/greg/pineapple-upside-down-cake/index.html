Pineapple Upside-Down Cake

A classic — brown sugar caramel base with pineapple rings and maraschino cherries

Category
Desserts
Pan
13×9

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks butter
  • 1 lb light brown sugar
  • 6 eggs, separated
  • Maraschino cherries
  • 1 large can sliced pineapples (reserve juice)
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 2 TBSP baking powder

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°.
  2. Melt butter and brown sugar together in the 13×9 pan. Layer pineapple slices with cherries in the center of each ring.
  3. Bowl #1: Combine egg yolks, reserved pineapple juice, white sugar, flour, and baking powder. Mix on low speed.
  4. Bowl #2: Beat egg whites until stiff peaks form.
  5. Fold whites into the yolk mixture from Bowl #1.
  6. Pour batter over the pineapple/brown sugar layer in the pan.
  7. Place a catch pan under the baking pan. Bake 40–45 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Run a knife around the edges; flip onto a tray immediately.
