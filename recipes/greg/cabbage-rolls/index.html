Cabbage Rolls

Lebanese-style stuffed cabbage — ground beef and rice simmered in pork stock

Category
Lebanese & Syrian
Serves
Large batch

Ingredients

For the Stock

  • 3 lbs country-style pork ribs (boil 55 min to make stock, then reserve)

For the Filling

  • 4 lbs ground chuck
  • 2.7 cups medium-grain rice
  • 2.7 TBSP salt
  • 2.7 TBSP Syrian Spice (see Syrian Spice Mix)
  • Black pepper
  • Dried mint leaves
  • Granulated garlic
  • 5.25 TBSP citric acid (added in last 15 minutes)
  • 1–2 heads cabbage
Yield: 1 lb beef = approx. 30 cabbage rolls.
Citric acid ratio: 4 lbs beef + 3 lbs pork = 7 lbs meat × 0.75 TBSP/lb = 5.25 TBSP citric acid.
Grape Leaves variation: 2.25 cups rice · 3 lbs ground chuck · 2.25 TBSP salt · 2.25 TBSP Syrian Spice · 2.25 TBSP citric acid (last 15 min). 1 lb beef = 3 oz grape leaves.

Directions

  1. Boil pork in a large pot of water for 55 minutes to make stock. Skim fat. Store stock and pork separately.
  2. Core cabbage and save stem for the bottom of the pot. Boil cabbage and peel off leaves as they loosen. De-vein leaves and cut in half.
  3. Mix ground chuck with 2.7 TBSP salt and 2.7 TBSP Syrian Spice. Mix in rice. Add cold water to keep the meat/rice mixture loose.
  4. Roll meat/rice mix in cabbage leaves; keep loose end of roll down.
  5. Place loose cabbage (core, veins, ripped leaves) at the bottom of the pot. Place reserved pork above. Season with garlic, mint, and black pepper.
  6. Layer cabbage rolls above pork, alternating orientation. Season with garlic, mint, and pepper between each layer.
  7. Place two plates on top of rolls. Fill pot with stock to 2" above plates.
  8. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium and cook 40 minutes.
  9. Add citric acid and bring back to a boil for a final 15 minutes.
