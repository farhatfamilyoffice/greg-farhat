Syrian Spice Mix

The essential spice blend for Lebanese and Syrian cooking

Category
Pantry
Yield
~2.25 lbs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb black pepper
  • 1 lb allspice
  • 3 TBSP cinnamon (approx. 1/44 of total mix)
Use in Cabbage Rolls, Kousa, Grape Leaves, and other Lebanese/Syrian dishes.

Directions

  1. Combine black pepper, allspice, and cinnamon.
  2. Mix well and store in an airtight container.
