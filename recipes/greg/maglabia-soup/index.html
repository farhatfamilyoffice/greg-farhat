Maglabia Soup

Whole chicken simmered with onions, cinnamon, and chickpeas

Category
Lebanese & Syrian
Serves
6–8

Ingredients

  • 5 lbs whole chicken
  • 3 lbs small onions
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1.5 TBSP salt
  • 1 TBSP white pepper
  • 16 cups water
  • 2 cans chickpeas (15–19 oz each)

Directions

  1. Place chicken in a large pot with 16 cups water. Cook at medium-high heat for 60 minutes.
  2. Remove chicken; debone and return chicken meat to broth.
  3. Add small onions; cook 45 minutes.
  4. Add cinnamon sticks, salt, and white pepper; cook 30 minutes.
  5. Add chickpeas; cook 10 minutes.
  6. Serve with maglabia balls, orzo, or rice.
