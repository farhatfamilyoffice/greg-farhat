Caesar Salad

Classic tableside Caesar dressing — made in a wooden bowl

Category
Salads

Ingredients

  • ¼–½ tsp salt
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 2 anchovy fillets
  • Dash dry mustard
  • ¼ tsp ground pepper
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 drops Tabasco
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 egg yolk
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 TBSP red wine vinegar
  • Romaine lettuce, washed and dried
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Croutons (optional)

Directions

  1. Use a wooden bowl and large spoon.
  2. Mash salt and garlic together with spoon until a paste forms.
  3. Add anchovies and mash into the garlic paste.
  4. Add dry mustard, ground pepper, Worcestershire, Tabasco, and lemon juice; mix well.
  5. Add egg yolk and mix.
  6. Slowly add olive oil while mixing.
  7. Add red wine vinegar and mix.
  8. Add romaine lettuce; toss to coat.
  9. Add Parmesan cheese and toss again. Add croutons if desired.
