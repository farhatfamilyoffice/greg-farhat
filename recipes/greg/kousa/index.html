Kousa — Stuffed Zucchini

Lebanese stuffed zucchini with ground beef, rice, and Syrian spice

Category
Lebanese & Syrian
Serves
Large batch

Ingredients

For the Stock

  • 3 lbs country-style pork ribs

For the Filling

  • 4 lbs ground chuck
  • 2.7 cups medium-grain rice
  • 2.7 TBSP salt
  • 2.7 TBSP Syrian Spice (see Syrian Spice Mix)
  • Black pepper
  • Dried mint leaves
  • Granulated garlic
  • 5.25 TBSP citric acid (added in last 15 minutes)
  • Kousa (Lebanese zucchini), cored — leave 1" at top for rice to expand
  • Small amount tomato sauce (added mid-cook)
Citric acid ratio: 4 lbs beef + 3 lbs pork = 7 lbs × 0.75 TBSP/lb = 5.25 TBSP.

Directions

  1. Put ground chuck in a bowl. Rinse rice and place in a separate bowl.
  2. Add Syrian Spice and salt to the rice; mix.
  3. Prepare a bowl of ice water.
  4. Add seasoned rice to the meat, then add cold water until the mixture is loose enough that your hand (like a claw) can run freely through it.
  5. Pack meat/rice mixture into cored kousa, leaving 1" at top for rice to expand.
  6. Stand kousa upright in a large pot. Add water to cover.
  7. Boil until pink is gone from meat, about 15–20 minutes. Skim fat.
  8. Reduce to medium heat; add mint, granulated garlic, and a small amount of tomato sauce. Cook 45 minutes.
  9. Return to a boil; add citric acid and cook a final 15 minutes.
