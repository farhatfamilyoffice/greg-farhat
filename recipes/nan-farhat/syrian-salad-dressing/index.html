Syrian Salad Dressing

Lebanese-style salad with lettuce, cucumber, and tomato in a bright lemon and mint dressing — serves 2–4

Section
Soups & Salads
Yield
2–4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 head lettuce
  • ¼ onion
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ cup Mazola oil
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 lemons, squeezed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Pepper to taste (⅛ pepper)
  • 1 tsp mint leaves

Directions

  1. Tear or chop lettuce. Slice onion, cucumber, and tomato and combine with lettuce.
  2. Whisk together Mazola oil, apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and mint leaves.
  3. Toss salad with dressing and serve immediately.
