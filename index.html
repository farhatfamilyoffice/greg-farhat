About Me

I’m Greg Farhat, a corporate real estate professional, Michigan real estate broker, and civic board member based in the Lansing area. My work and interests center on business growth, community development, and the people and places that shape our lives.

My career began in commercial construction with The Christman Company, ranked No. 68 nationally on Engineering News-Record’s 2026 Top 400 Contractors list. There, I was involved in more than $250 million of construction and real estate development, working on projects ranging from data centers and schools to historic restorations. That experience grounded me in the details of how buildings come together and the importance of good execution across a large team.

Today, I am responsible for real estate strategy and corporate initiatives at Jackson, a top-rated financial services company. At Jackson, I have overseen more than $250 million of construction and real estate development, and I oversee more than one million square feet of office space and data centers.

I earned a bachelor’s degree in construction management from Michigan State University and an MBA from Northwood University. I have also pursued executive education through the University of Michigan’s Ross School of Business and Imperial College London, including study in London, Shenzhen, and Hong Kong. My perspective brings together construction, real estate, and business, balancing practical details with long-term priorities.

Family business is another part of my life, and I’m particularly proud of what my wife, Jess, has built. Jessica Farhat, MSN, NP-C, is a board-certified nurse practitioner and the founder and operator of NECTAR body•face•skin, a boutique medical aesthetics practice based in DeWitt and expanding into Jackson, Michigan. She also provides consulting nationally. She brings extensive experience in medical, surgical, and cosmetic dermatology to a business built around clinical expertise, thoughtful care, and natural results.

Jess’s expertise has earned recognition beyond Michigan. She is one of only 40 providers selected globally as a ZO® Skin Health Ambassador and has trained directly with Dr. Zein Obagi. NECTAR is also a ZO Sapphire Circle Club member. Under her leadership, the practice was named one of the 2025 Michigan 50 Companies to Watch by Michigan Celebrates Small Business and earned Lansing City Pulse’s Best Med Spa and Best Facial awards in 2024. I’m proud of both the practice she has created and the team she continues to develop.

I hold my Michigan real estate broker’s license through Jameson Real Estate Services, and we own commercial real estate.

I serve on the boards of the Lansing Regional Chamber of Commerce and LEAP (Lansing Economic Area Partnership). My community involvement has also included Allen Neighborhood Center, the Boys & Girls Club of Lansing, and Ele’s Place.

Aviation is another important part of my life. I earned my private pilot license in 2025 and own a Piper aircraft hangared at Capital Region International Airport (KLAN). General aviation sits alongside my work in real estate, business, and community as a distinct part of who I am.

We’re also proud to see our children developing their own interests and careers. Our daughter, Olivia, recently became a licensed esthetician in Michigan. She currently works in a support role at NECTAR while continuing her in-house training as an esthetician. Our son Sean is completing his senior year at Lansing Catholic High School and plans to follow my path by pursuing a construction management degree. He has been accepted to Northern Michigan University and is also considering Ferris State University and Michigan State University.

Beyond work, I value family and the traditions that connect generations. My grandfather, Leo A. Farhat, whom we called Gido, was a prominent Michigan attorney. My grandmother, Virginia M. Farhat, whom we called Tete, was the glue of our family through her grace, love, and humor. Together, they started the traditions we continue to build on today. Preserving our family tree, recipes, and shared stories is a way of honoring them and carrying that foundation forward for our children and future generations.
