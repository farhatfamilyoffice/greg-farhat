About Greg Farhat

Greg Farhat is a corporate real estate leader based in Lansing, Michigan, responsible for aligning workplace infrastructure, facilities operations, and capital projects with enterprise strategy at Jackson — one of the largest financial services organizations in the United States.

At Jackson, Greg leads a national real estate portfolio supporting thousands of associates. His work focuses on ensuring development initiatives, facilities, and workplace environments enable long-term business performance, operational resilience, and an exceptional associate experience. This requires close collaboration with executive leadership and cross-functional partners across finance, legal, human resources, and technology to align infrastructure decisions with broader organizational priorities.

Through roles in construction management and corporate real estate leadership, Greg has contributed to more than $1 billion in capital deployment across development projects, infrastructure initiatives, and enterprise workplace investments.

Prior to Jackson, Greg served as a Project Manager with The Christman Company, where he worked on over $250 million in commercial development projects across Michigan.

In addition to his corporate role, Greg is a private real estate investor and operator through Farhat Family Office, focused on building and stewarding a portfolio of commercial real estate holdings in Mid-Michigan.

Greg holds a degree from Michigan State University and is actively involved in the greater Lansing community, including support for Ele’s Place Capital Region and civic and charitable initiatives in Mid-Michigan.
